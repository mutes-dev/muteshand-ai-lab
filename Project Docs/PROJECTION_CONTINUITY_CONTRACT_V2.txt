## Purpose

Defines continuity semantics for projections, hydration, streaming, polling, and rendered workflow views.

This contract ensures:
- stable workflow continuity
- deterministic projection synchronization
- correct hydration behavior
- prevention of stale or divergent projections
- deterministic reconciliation behavior

Projection synchronization follows the sequencing defined in:
ORCHESTRATION_AND_EXECUTION_SEQUENCE_CONTRACT_V2

---

## Core Principle

Lifecycle Authority owns lifecycle truth.

Projection Continuity owns synchronization semantics for rendered/projected state.

Projection systems are:
- read models
- cached views
- continuity layers

They are NOT authoritative lifecycle sources.

---

## 1. Projection Model

Projection state represents:
- rendered workflow continuity
- accumulated workflow view state
- synchronized execution visibility

Projection state is NOT authoritative execution truth.

---

## 2. Authoritative Sources

Authoritative lifecycle truth originates ONLY from:
- Lifecycle Authority Layer

Projection systems MUST synchronize FROM authority.

Projection synchronization follows the canonical commit ordering defined in:
ORCHESTRATION_AND_EXECUTION_SEQUENCE_CONTRACT_V2

Projection systems MUST NOT synthesize lifecycle truth.

---

## 3. Continuity Ownership

Projection Continuity owns:
- hydration continuity
- event accumulation semantics
- projection merge semantics
- projection replacement rules
- stale projection detection
- projection invalidation
- terminal reconciliation semantics
- stream/poll synchronization ordering

### Workflow Identity Continuity Boundary

Projection continuity preserves:
- visibility continuity
- synchronization continuity
- hydration continuity

Projection continuity does NOT define:
- workflow existence
- workflow identity truth
- lifecycle ownership

Projection continuity MUST synchronize FROM:
- authoritative workflow identity
- authoritative lifecycle state

Workflow identity truth belongs to Lifecycle Authority and persistence-backed validated reconstruction.
Projection continuity preserves visibility/hydration continuity only.
Projection continuity must not infer workflow existence.

---

## 4. Hydration Semantics

Hydration follows the sequencing defined in:
ORCHESTRATION_AND_EXECUTION_SEQUENCE_CONTRACT_V2

Hydration MUST:
- reconstruct latest valid projection state
- preserve continuity across reconnect/reload
- reconcile from authoritative lifecycle state

Hydration MUST NOT:
- invent missing lifecycle state
- infer execution completion
- overwrite authority truth

---

## 5. Event Accumulation Semantics

Projection systems MAY accumulate:
- step events
- outputs
- trace entries
- stream events

Accumulation MUST:
- preserve deterministic ordering
- avoid duplicate application
- avoid invalid rollback of newer state

---

## 6. Stream Synchronization Rules

Streams are transport-only.

Stream synchronization follows the sequencing defined in:
ORCHESTRATION_AND_EXECUTION_SEQUENCE_CONTRACT_V2

Stream events:
- update projections
- do NOT define authoritative truth

Late or stale stream events MUST NOT overwrite newer projection state.

---

## 7. Polling Synchronization Rules

Polling may provide backend-authored snapshots derived from authoritative lifecycle/runtime state.

Polling follows the synchronization ordering defined in:
ORCHESTRATION_AND_EXECUTION_SEQUENCE_CONTRACT_V2

Polling MAY:
- refresh projections
- repair stale continuity

Polling MUST NOT:
- synthesize lifecycle transitions
- create conflicting continuity state

Polling refreshes projections but does not own authority.

Authoritative refresh snapshots supersede stale local continuity state.

If projection divergence is detected:
- authoritative refresh state becomes canonical
- stale continuity projections MUST reconcile
- invalid local continuity MUST NOT override authoritative refreshes

---

## 8. Merge Semantics

Projection updates MUST follow deterministic merge rules.

Newer valid projection data supersedes older projection data.

Projection merge MUST NOT:
- remove valid accumulated continuity
- invalidate newer synchronized state
- revert terminal projections incorrectly

---

## 9. Terminal Reconciliation

Terminal continuity anchors:
- COMPLETED = immutable terminal
- CANCELLED = immutable terminal
- FAILED = recoverable terminal

BLOCKED is recoverable non-terminal/wait continuity anchor, NOT terminal.

Terminal projections MUST NOT revert unless Lifecycle Authority and controlling contracts authorize a legal recovery/re-execution path.

Projection continuity must not synthesize retry legality or recovery authority.

---

## 10. Projection Invalidation

Projection invalidation MAY occur when:
- workflow re-executes
- plan invalidates execution
- lifecycle authority invalidates continuity
- stale projection detected

Invalid projections MUST:
- refresh from authority
- rebuild deterministically

---

## 11. Stale Projection Detection

Projection systems MUST detect:
- stale lifecycle snapshots
- invalid stream ordering
- missing continuity segments
- projection divergence

Detected stale projections MUST trigger:
- refresh
- reconciliation
- rehydration

---

## 12. Multi-Project Continuity

Projection continuity MUST remain isolated per workflow_id.

Cross-project projection contamination is prohibited.

### Multi-Workflow Continuity

Projection continuity MUST support:
- simultaneous workflow continuity
- workflow-scoped hydration continuity
- workflow-scoped synchronization continuity

Projection continuity MUST NOT:
- merge workflow identity continuity
- infer authoritative workflow ownership
- synthesize workflow existence

---

## 13. Consistency Guarantee

Projection continuity MUST guarantee:
- stable rendered continuity
- deterministic synchronization
- no invalid lifecycle combinations
- no disappearing valid workflows
- no invalid ACTIVE/COMPLETED coexistence

---

## 14. Prohibited Patterns

The following are prohibited:
- frontend lifecycle synthesis
- projection-derived lifecycle authority
- stream-authoritative lifecycle state
- uncontrolled projection replacement
- invalid projection rollback
- projection overwrite without ordering validation

---

## Final Principle

Lifecycle Authority defines truth.

Projection Continuity defines synchronized visibility of truth.

Rendering systems consume continuity projections but do NOT define authority.

---

## Eventual Consistency Model

Projection continuity uses eventual consistency semantics.

Temporary projection/runtime divergence MAY occur during:
- streaming
- retry
- mutation
- pause/resume
- synchronization refresh

Projection systems MUST eventually converge to authoritative runtime/lifecycle state.

Permanent divergence is prohibited.

---

## Convergence Boundary

Projection continuity governs:
- synchronization semantics
- continuity behavior
- projection reconciliation

Projection continuity does NOT govern:
- lifecycle finalization sequencing
- restart choreography
- terminalization authority
- recovery ordering

These concerns are governed separately by:
SYSTEM_CONVERGENCE_AND_RECOVERY_CONTRACT_V2

Projection convergence is strictly downstream-only per:
ORCHESTRATION_AND_EXECUTION_SEQUENCE_CONTRACT_V2

MUTATION_LEGALITY_AND_RUNTIME_ISOLATION_CONTRACT_V2

EXECUTION_IDENTITY_AND_REPLAY_CONTRACT_V2

Stale execution projections are invalidatable per:
RUNTIME_REGISTRY_AND_EXECUTION_COORDINATION_CONTRACT_V2

Replay continuity is distinct from recovery continuity per:
EXECUTION_IDENTITY_AND_REPLAY_CONTRACT_V2

## 15. Observability Continuity Semantics

### Observability Continuity

Dashboard and observability projection continuity follows the same synchronization semantics as canonical projection continuity.

Observability continuity includes:
- dashboard projection continuity
- websocket stream continuity
- aggregated visibility continuity
- runtime discovery continuity

Observability continuity does NOT include:
- authority continuity
- lifecycle ownership continuity
- orchestration coordination continuity

---

### Dashboard Projection Continuity

Dashboard projections MUST synchronize from authoritative sources.

Dashboard projection continuity MUST:
- preserve passive read-model visibility
- reconcile from authoritative refresh snapshots
- remain deterministic across reconnect/reload

Dashboard projection continuity MUST NOT:
- synthesize workflow existence
- infer lifecycle state transitions
- derive orchestration ownership from stream continuity

---

### Explicit Workflow Attachment Continuity

Focused workflow interaction continuity remains:
- explicitly scoped
- workflow-bound
- separate from dashboard observability continuity

A focused workflow screen continuity:
- is tied to an explicitly attached workflow_id
- does NOT auto-attach to newly discovered workflows
- does NOT derive attachment from observability feeds alone

---

### Websocket Continuity Separation

Websocket stream continuity is:
- transport-layer continuity
- projection-delivery continuity
- synchronization-layer continuity

Websocket continuity MUST NOT:
- become lifecycle authority continuity
- define workflow existence continuity
- infer orchestration ownership continuity

---

### Observability Continuity vs Authority Continuity

Observability continuity governs:
- what the frontend sees
- how projections render across reconnects
- how dashboards aggregate and display

Authority continuity governs:
- what the system knows to be true
- lifecycle state ownership
- orchestration coordination ownership

These continuities are:
- architecturally separate
- MUST NOT be conflated
- MUST NOT be substituted for one another

---

## Prohibited Continuity Patterns

The following are prohibited:
- inferring focused workflow attachment from dashboard observability
- treating websocket stream continuity as lifecycle authority continuity
- reconstructing workflow ownership from observability projection continuity
- allowing dashboard auto-discovery to drive workflow interaction scope

## Cancellation Continuity Semantics

Projection continuity for CANCELLED workflows:
- MUST remain stable
- MUST NOT revert unless explicitly invalidated
- MUST display immutable terminal state

Cancellation invalidates all active execution instances.

Projection continuity MUST reflect terminalization as continuity anchor.

WORKFLOW_CANCELLATION_AND_TERMINALIZATION_CONTRACT_V1

## Recoverable Terminal Continuity Semantics

Projection continuity for FAILED workflows:
- MAY expose retry lineage visibility
- MUST preserve execution lineage history
- MUST remain non-authoritative

Recoverable terminal states MAY support:
- retry (creates NEW execution lineage)
- reconciliation (future)
- adaptive continuation (future)

Projection continuity MUST NOT:
- synthesize retry legality
- authorize retry
- restore execution context

Recoverable terminal semantics are governed by:
STATE_TRANSITIONS_CONTRACT_V2

---

## Retry Lineage Continuity Semantics

Retry creates new execution lineage.

Projection continuity during retry:
- MUST invalidate previous execution projections
- MUST generate new execution projections
- MUST update execution lineage
- MUST preserve historical continuity

Projection continuity MUST NOT:
- continue stale execution projections
- resurrect previous execution context
- synthesize execution identity

Retry creates new execution lineage.
Projection continuity displays lineage and invalidates stale execution projections.
Projection continuity must not resurrect execution context or synthesize execution identity.

Retry continuity semantics are governed by:
EXECUTION_IDENTITY_AND_REPLAY_CONTRACT_V2
