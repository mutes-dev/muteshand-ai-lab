# AI LAB — STEP RESOLUTION CONTRACT V1

## PURPOSE

Defines how PLAN STEPs are transformed into EXECUTION STEPs.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Resolution = converting intent (WHAT) into execution (HOW)

---

## RESPONSIBILITY

Orchestrator / Agent MUST:

* select appropriate tool
* construct tool_call
* inject into step

---

## FLOW

PLAN STEP → RESOLUTION → EXECUTION STEP

---

## RESOLUTION TIMING

Resolution MUST occur before execution.

Resolution MAY be performed per step (lazy resolution).

---

### REQUIRED RULES

* A step MUST be resolved BEFORE it transitions from PENDING → ACTIVE
* Resolution MUST produce a valid STEP_SCHEMA-compliant step
* Resolution MUST NOT occur after execution begins

---

### RECOMMENDED MODEL

Lazy resolution (per-step):

* Resolve step immediately before execution
* Allows plan edits before execution
* Avoids unnecessary resolution work

---

### CONSTRAINT

Unresolved PLAN STEP MUST NOT enter execution.

---

## RULES

* Resolution MUST occur before validation
* Resolution MUST produce valid STEP_SCHEMA
* Resolution MUST NOT modify step purpose
* Resolution MUST respect dependencies and context

---

## OUTPUT

Result MUST be a valid STEP as defined in STEP_SCHEMA_CONTRACT

---

## VALIDATION

Only resolved steps may be validated and executed.

---

## FINAL PRINCIPLE

Execution requires complete definition.
Resolution completes the step.
