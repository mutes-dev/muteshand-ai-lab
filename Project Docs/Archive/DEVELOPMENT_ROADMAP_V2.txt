## 5. MAJOR KNOWN RISKS

- **Lifecycle authority drift:** Multiple layers may claim lifecycle ownership
- **Replay/recovery confusion:** Recovery may accidentally replay execution
- **Projection authority leakage:** Projections or GUI may synthesize lifecycle truth
- **Stale-state overwrite:** Old persistence may overwrite current authority on restart
- **Retry mutation races:** Concurrent mutation during retry window
- **Frontend lifecycle synthesis:** Frontend infers or restores lifecycle state from projections

---

## 6. DEFINITION OF “SYSTEM COMPLETE”

The system is considered complete when:

- Deterministic execution is stable
- Orchestration sequences correctly without owning authority
- Recovery survives restart safely without replay
- Projections remain derived-only
- GUI cannot violate runtime legality
- Retries are isolated correctly
- Illegal mutations are impossible
- Convergence is stable and downward-only
- Production monitoring exists
- Deployment/recovery is operationally safe
