## 6. REMAINING AUDIT RISK AREAS

### Risk 1: Unclassified Mutation Legality Contract
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Mitigation:** Add MUTATION_LEGALITY_AND_RUNTIME_ISOLATION_CONTRACT_V1.txt to RUNTIME / EXECUTION CONTRACTS classification under LIFECYCLE or EXECUTION STRUCTURE authority.

### Risk 2: Phantom STREAMING_CONTRACT_V1 Reference
**Severity:** LOW
**Mitigation:** Update KNOWN_ISSUES_V2 references to point to OBSERVABILITY_AND_DASHBOARD_ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACT_V1 or INTERFACE_CONTRACT_V1 instead of non-existent STREAMING_CONTRACT_V1.

### Risk 3: ROADMAP_V2_TEMP Layer Status Misinterpretation
**Severity:** LOW-MEDIUM
**Details:** Many layers show NOT STARTED despite being implemented and verified per SYSTEM_STATE_V2. The interpretation model correctly classifies ROADMAP_V2_TEMP as progression authority, but the visual gap is large. An inattentive auditor might conflate roadmap status with implementation reality.
**Mitigation:** Pre-audit briefing to internalize precedence rules; no document change required.

### Risk 4: Accepted Operational Limitations Not Indexed
**Severity:** LOW
**Details:** Specific accepted limitations (foreground/projection divergence, test degradation, LLM latency) exist in SYSTEM_STATE_V2 and KNOWN_ISSUES_V2 but are not explicitly listed in the interpretation model's false-positive prevention section.
**Mitigation:** Cross-reference requirement for auditors; optional hardening to add explicit entries.

### Risk 5: Cooperative Orchestration Semantics Expectation
**Severity:** LOW
**Details:** DECISION_LOG_V2 clarifies orchestration isolation is cooperative, not mutex-based. The interpretation model preserves this semantics but does not prominently headline it. An auditor expecting strict mutex semantics could misclassify cooperative behavior as isolation drift.
**Mitigation:** No change needed; semantics are preserved and documented.
