Classification: Architecture / contract foundation; not implementation approval

1. Purpose

This contract defines grounding and false-success validators used to prevent unsupported, ungrounded, dummy, stale, unresolved, or falsely successful outputs.

2. Authority boundaries

Validators do not create lifecycle states.
Validators do not move lifecycle authority.
Validators do not move governance authority.
Validators do not make frontend/projection authoritative.
Validators may influence finalization, continuation, replan, block, or fail only through approved governance/runtime paths.

3. Validator classes

schema_validator:
  Detects missing/malformed tool calls, wrong result shape, missing required fields.

unresolved_reference_validator:
  Detects unresolved references in final/search/tool inputs.

dependency_grounding_validator:
  Ensures downstream steps use required upstream outputs/evidence.

source_grounding_validator:
  Ensures claims cite or map to source evidence.

numeric_grounding_validator:
  Ensures numeric/table answers are recomputable from row/column/cell refs.

artifact_existence_validator:
  Checks claimed write/edit artifacts actually exist or changed.

dummy_result_detector:
  Detects placeholder/dummy outputs such as example.com, <response>, empty pages, templated success.

irrelevant_result_detector:
  Detects evidence/source that does not answer the requested question.

ambiguity_classifier:
  Detects multiple plausible refs/answers where safe auto-choice is not possible.

final_answer_evidence_validator:
  Ensures final answer satisfies evidence requirements.

4. Validator severity

advisory:
  Warning only.

blocking:
  Prevents finalization or next unsafe action until resolved.

replan_required:
  Requires continuation/replan.

approval_required:
  Requires user/governance approval/clarification.

failure_candidate:
  May recommend governance failure path, but does not itself commit lifecycle failure.

5. P0 validators

Approved as foundation P0:
- schema_validator;
- unresolved_reference_validator;
- dependency_grounding_validator;
- artifact_existence_validator;
- dummy_result_detector for obvious placeholders.

6. P1 validators

Approved as staged/advisory first:
- source_grounding_validator;
- numeric_grounding_validator;
- ambiguity_classifier;
- irrelevant_result_detector;
- final_answer_evidence_validator.

7. Finalization rules

If evidence_requirement is present, finalization must verify evidence exists, dependency refs used, unresolved refs absent, claims map to evidence where required, and numeric results ground to table refs where required.

If validation fails, return not_found, ambiguous, unsupported, blocked, partial, or replan_required as appropriate. Do not present unsupported answer as successful.

8. Lifecycle/governance effects

A validator may block finalization through an approved runtime/governance path.
A validator may not independently mark workflow FAILED unless GOVERNANCE_CONTRACT allows that path.
Any future expansion of validator lifecycle effects must cross-reference GOVERNANCE_CONTRACT_V3.

9. Non-goals

This contract does not approve LLM judge as broad lifecycle authority, learning/adaptation, new lifecycle states, frontend authority, or broad auto-failure from weak heuristics.
