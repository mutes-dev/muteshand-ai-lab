## 8. Sub-Dev Working Rules

Bryan's working preferences:

- **Audit-first by default.** Understand before implementing.
- **One issue at a time.** Finish one before starting the next.
- **Single copyblock prompts.** Do not create Frankenstein prompts that merge multiple unrelated tasks.
- **No prompt changes without explicit flag.** If you change a prompt, flag it and get approval.
- **Docs updated at issue/session end.** Do not leave docs stale.

## 9. What NOT to Do

- Do not modify runtime contracts without SA approval.
- Do not make roadmap decisions silently.
- Do not copy reference code directly without item-level approval.
- Do not adopt a reference orchestrator, agent loop, or execution engine.
- Do not implement timeout/circuit breaker without strict GUI/runtime failure-path validation.
- Do not activate learning or adaptive behavior without explicit Sprint 12 entry conditions.
- Do not treat projection or frontend as authoritative.
- Do not update SYSTEM_STATE_V2 with roadmap commitments or future plans.
- Do not add new paid service integrations without spend and approval gates.
- Do not implement browser automation without sandbox, privacy, and spend gates.
- Do not leave gaps in a vague "deferred" state; every gap must be owned, blocked, or rejected.
