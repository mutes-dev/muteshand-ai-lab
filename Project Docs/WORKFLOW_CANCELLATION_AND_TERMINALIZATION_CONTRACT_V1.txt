# 3. TERMINAL STATE CLASSIFICATION

## Immutable Terminal

States:

* COMPLETED
* CANCELLED

Immutable terminal states do NOT imply archival state.

Lifecycle state and retention state are independent.

Cancellation does not imply archival.

Terminalization does not imply retention.

Retention actions, if introduced, do NOT alter lifecycle truth.

Immutable terminal states:

* do NOT support retry
* do NOT support continuation
* do NOT support structural mutation
* MAY support observability and inspection

## Recoverable Terminal

States:

* FAILED
* BLOCKED (future)

Recoverable terminal states MAY support:

* retry
* reconciliation
* adaptive continuation
* future branch healing

Explicitly define:

FAILED is recoverable terminal.
CANCELLED is immutable terminal.
COMPLETED is immutable terminal.
