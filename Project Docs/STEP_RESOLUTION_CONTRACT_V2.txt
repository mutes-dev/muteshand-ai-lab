## PURPOSE

Defines how PLAN STEPs are transformed into EXECUTION STEPs.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Resolution = converting intent (WHAT) into execution (HOW)

Resolution prepares executable inputs but does NOT execute tools.
Resolution does NOT bypass dependency validation, governance, mutation legality, or system_entry.

---

## RESPONSIBILITY

Orchestrator / Agent MUST:

* select appropriate tool
* construct tool_call
* inject into step

Resolution MUST remain within bounded authority:
- Resolution prepares structure.
- Resolution does NOT execute.
- Resolution does NOT govern.
- Resolution does NOT claim lifecycle authority.

---

## FLOW

PLAN STEP → RESOLUTION → EXECUTION STEP → VALIDATION → GOVERNANCE → system_entry → EXECUTION

---

## RESOLUTION TIMING

Resolution MUST occur before execution.

Resolution MAY be performed per step (lazy resolution).

---

### REQUIRED RULES

* A step MUST be resolved BEFORE it transitions from PENDING → ACTIVE
* Resolution MUST produce a valid STEP_SCHEMA_CONTRACT_V2-compliant step
* Resolution MUST NOT occur after execution begins
* Resolution MUST NOT bypass validation
* Resolution MUST NOT bypass governance

---

### RECOMMENDED MODEL

Lazy resolution (per-step):

* Resolve step immediately before execution
* Allows plan edits before execution
* Avoids unnecessary resolution work

---

### CONSTRAINT

Unresolved PLAN STEP MUST NOT enter execution.

---

## RULES

* Resolution MUST occur before validation
* Resolution MUST produce valid STEP_SCHEMA per STEP_SCHEMA_CONTRACT_V2
* Resolution MUST NOT modify step purpose
* Resolution MUST respect dependencies and context
* Resolution MUST NOT infer broad semantic dependencies at runtime
* Resolution MUST NOT bypass dependency validation per DEPENDENCY_MODEL_CONTRACT_V2
* Resolution MUST NOT bypass mutation legality per MUTATION_LEGALITY_AND_RUNTIME_ISOLATION_CONTRACT_V2
* Resolution MUST NOT bypass system_entry per CORE_SYSTEM_ENTRY_PIPELINE_ARCHITECTURE_V1

---

## OUTPUT

Result MUST be a valid STEP as defined in STEP_SCHEMA_CONTRACT_V2.

---

## MISSING / INVALID INPUT HANDLING

If resolution encounters missing or invalid inputs:

* resolution MUST NOT proceed with incomplete data
* system MUST block, escalate, or fail according to:
  - GOVERNANCE_CONTRACT_V3
  - EXECUTION_RUNTIME_GOVERNANCE_CONTRACT_V2
  - LIFECYCLE_AUTHORITY_CONTRACT_V2

---

## VALIDATION

Only resolved steps may be validated and executed.

Validation MUST check:
- STEP_SCHEMA_CONTRACT_V2 compliance
- tool_call format per INTERFACE_CONTRACT_V2
- dependency correctness per DEPENDENCY_MODEL_CONTRACT_V2

---

## FINAL PRINCIPLE

Execution requires complete definition.
Resolution completes the step definition.
Resolution does NOT execute.
Resolution does NOT own execution truth, lifecycle truth, or governance authority.
